# KIT24 Undertray Development Log

This repository documents my aerodynamic design work on the undertray of KA-RaceIng's KIT24, developed during my time as Development Engineer and Subteam Lead Aerodynamics.

## Structure

Each undertray component has two associated files:

- **`<Component>.pdf`** — a concept and season summary explaining the design reasoning: what was tried, what worked, what didn't, and why. Start here for an overview of each component.
- **`KIT24_Undertray<Component>_Development_log.txt`** — the full, unedited iteration log for that component, exported directly from our internal team wiki. It records every CAD revision with its simulated cl/cd values and engineer's notes, and is included as the primary source behind the summaries in the PDFs. Some entries are in German and use internal team shorthand (e.g. MW = Mainwing, VG = Vortex Generators, WP = Wheelplate, FP = Floor Panel), and image references use our internal wiki's syntax rather than standard Markdown, so they won't render on GitHub.

`kit24_ut.png` shows the complete assembled undertray for reference.
